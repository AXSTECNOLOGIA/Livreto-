## Localization notes
- "onça" → "jaguar" (not "leopard"/"cheetah" — distinct species; jaguar is the only *Panthera* native to Brazil).
- EN lines are ±10% the PT syllable count to fit the same shot timing; SC06 narrator line is the longest (~4.5 s) — keep the final shot ≥ 6 s.
- Lip sync: EN mouth shapes will not match PT animation. See risk R-03 in the diagnostic report.
